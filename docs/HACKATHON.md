# GTM Hackathon Berlin (Halloween edition): facts

Hosted by Arvana and GTMengineer.dev. Saturday 31 October 2026, Arvana office, Rosenstrasse 17, 10178 Berlin.

## Goal of the event
Generate real pipeline for an existing product (Arvana's or our own). Not about building a new product. Open format: any novel GTM solution counts. The listed challenges are optional inspiration.

## Schedule (31 Oct 2026)
- 11:00 check-in, 11:30 welcome and Arvana presentation
- 12:00 hacking starts
- 12:30 team submission deadline (team form)
- 17:00 submission deadline: a 2-minute video walkthrough (Loom, YouTube or similar) of the GTM workflow. No late submissions.
- 17:00 to 17:30 jury review, 17:30 to 18:30 prizes, 18:30 party

## Judging (25% each)
Business Impact, Tech, Works end-to-end, Pitch.
Jury: Nils, Arne Unkrig (Arvana), Emanuel Morhard (GTMengineer.dev).
Prize pool 2,000 EUR. Split and sponsor prizes were still "coming soon".

## About Arvana
B2B growth agency with two engines: Outreach (AI-personalized email, LinkedIn, AI agents) and Performance marketing (Google, LinkedIn, Meta, Microsoft).

## Suggested challenges (from the kick-off slides)
1. Find the dark TAM: companies no lead database knows, hidden in directories, portals and trade-fair lists. Scrape and enrich them into a lead list with decision makers. Only leads that are NOT in AI Ark count. Example niches: bakeries and butchers, car washes, IT service providers, doctors and pharmacies, family offices and LPs, asset managers (incl. portfolio size).
2. Make ChatGPT recommend Arvana (GEO). No blog or Reddit spam.
3. Creative factory: Meta ad creatives on autopilot.
4. Own idea or own startup.

## Constraints we must respect
- Cold B2B email to German recipients is legally risky (UWG). Prefer LinkedIn via Needle. Email stays drafted and verified only.
- New inboxes need warmup, so real email sends are not realistic today.
- Use only public professional data. Do not scrape private attendee registrations.
